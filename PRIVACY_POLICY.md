# Privacy Policy: Pocket Ledger

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Pocket Ledger is a personal expense and budget tracker. You can use it fully offline with no account.

**Without an account:** everything you enter (transactions, budgets, recurring items, settings) is stored only on your device. We collect nothing.

**If you create an account (optional):**
- We collect your **email address** to identify your account, and the **financial entries you save in the app** (amounts, categories, notes, dates, budgets) so they can be backed up and synced between your devices.
- This data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), encrypted in transit and at rest, and protected by rules that allow only you to read it. Google acts as our service provider; see Google's Firebase privacy terms.
- We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for tracking or analytics. We do not read your entries.

**Backups and exports:** "Back up to a file" and "Copy as CSV" create copies you control. Please store them safely.

**Deleting your data:** Settings > Delete account permanently removes your account and all cloud data. Settings > Delete all data clears entries from your device (and from your account if signed in). You can also request deletion by emailing YOUR_EMAIL_HERE from your account address.

**Retention:** Cloud data is kept until you delete it or your account.

**Children:** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Disclaimer:** The app is a record-keeping tool and does not provide financial advice.
