# Store listing draft

**App name (30 chars max):** Pocket Ledger: Budget Tracker

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Simple, private budget tracker. Daily spend limit, recurring bills, sync.

**Full description:**
Pocket Ledger helps you see where your money goes, with no ads and no bank logins.

• Log expenses and income in seconds, or straight from a home-screen widget or app-icon shortcut
• Clear monthly overview with a spending-by-category chart
• Set monthly budgets per category and see progress at a glance
• Browse and edit past months
• Choose your currency symbol
• Light and dark mode
• Export your data as CSV
• Daily "safe to spend" number from your budgets
• Recurring bills and income added automatically
• Back up to a file, or sign in with email to sync across your phones
• Private: works fully offline without an account; nothing is sold or shared

Pocket Ledger is a personal record-keeping tool and does not provide financial advice.
